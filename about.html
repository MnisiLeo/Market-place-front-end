About SM Properties

Connecting buyers and sellers of property and livestock across Eswatini.

WHO WE ARE

Connecting Opportunities. Closing Deals.

SM Properties and Livestock Brokers is a brokerage company that connects sellers and buyers of property and livestock, facilitating transactions on their behalf and earning a commission for successful deals.

The company acts as a professional middleman, making it easier, faster and more reliable for people to buy and sell property and livestock in Eswatini.

Our role is to identify opportunities, connect the right people and help facilitate the transaction from the initial agreement through to completion.

Our Mission

To create reliable connections between buyers and sellers while making property and livestock transactions more convenient, transparent and efficient.
